Google Calendar Integration

Open Google Calendar on your PC → Settings for your selected family calendar → Scroll to "Integrate Calendar" → Paste the Embed iframe Code or public sharing link below.

Why are shared calendars/holidays missing?

By default, Google's simple iframe only shows your primary personal calendar. To show your spouse's schedules, combined family events, and holidays:

  1. In Google Calendar settings (PC), click the "Customize" button right above your embed HTML block.
  2. On the left column of that Customizer, look for "Calendars to display".
  3. Check the checkboxes for your Holidays, Birthdays, and any shared Family calendars.
  4. Copy the newly generated Customize embed code at the top of that screen, and paste it here!
